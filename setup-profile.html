Setup Google Profile

Setting up a Google Profile in Chrome helps sync your data, manage bookmarks, passwords, and personalize your browsing experience.

Creating a Profile

  • Click your profile icon in the top-right corner of Chrome.
  • Select “Add” and log into your Google account.

Customizing Your Profile

Assign a name, profile picture, and choose a color theme. Enable sync to save bookmarks, history, and settings across devices.

Manage profiles anytime via chrome://settings/people.
